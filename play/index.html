The Brush Is the Sword

I told my master painting is boring. He handed me a brush and pointed at the straw.

Grinding the ink

One minute a round, with sound.

Cut the straw

Drag across a straw target to cut it. The drag is a real brush stroke, so it runs dry: lift and start a new one.

Spare the lanterns

The red lanterns belong to the master. Two targets in one stroke count extra.

Three mistakes

Let straw reach you or cut a lantern three times, and the master shows you what a brush is for.
